## gymcoach
GymBro's coach reasoning (`app/src/lib/coach/`) adapts the design of gymcoach: structured reason codes rendered separately from the decision, a stall test over per-session estimated 1RM, a deload recommendation from several stalled lifts, a priority selector for a single home insight, and a "what your coach sees" summary. The code was rewritten for GymBro; no source files were copied.

Source: https://github.com/gymcoach-app/gymcoach
